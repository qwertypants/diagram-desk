- **Refresh:** substantive evidence or explanation needs updating.
- **Narrow:** available sources support a smaller claim.
- **Historical:** retain the lesson with a clear date and historical framing.
- **Retain:** no change; explain the evidence and next review date if known.
- **Investigate overlap:** compare reader decisions and propose route-safe consolidation; no removal is implied.
- **Review state unknown:** metadata is absent; no review is inferred.
