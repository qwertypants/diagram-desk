Parallel research helps when independent questions have clear owners and the final synthesis can retain their evidence and uncertainty.

## The problem

A research request has independent lines of investigation that exceed one context window.

## Read the architecture

Read the assignments, evidence returns, and coverage review as separate responsibilities.

<DiagramEmbed slug="coordinated-research" title={'Research agents with owned work'} />

### Walk through it

#### 1. Define owned work

The lead decomposes the research question into bounded, independent assignments with required evidence and a shared deadline.

#### 2. Gather independent findings

A source researcher and counterevidence researcher work in separate contexts. Both return findings with source references, not only conclusions.

#### 3. Review and synthesize

Coverage review combines findings and identifies gaps. Additional work needs remaining budget. A failed worker triggers a bounded retry or an explicit limitation in the final answer.

## Design decisions

- Parallelize independent research rather than every reasoning step.
- Keep one owner for the synthesized answer.

## Failure modes

- Overlapping assignments waste tokens and repeat evidence.
- A synthesis can lose uncertainty or contradictory findings.

## Key takeaways

- More workers help only when work can be usefully divided.
