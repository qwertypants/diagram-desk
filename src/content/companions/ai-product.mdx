The model is one dependency inside an application. The application still owns user access, durable state, job status, and the meaning of a completed operation.

For a workspace assistant answering "What changed in Project Cedar this week?", document excerpts become model context. Workspace membership and saved summary status belong in durable application records. A generated claim that work was saved does not prove a record exists.

Start at **Web / mobile** and follow the authorized request into **Application**. Then compare the direct AI path with the **Job Queue** path.

**AI orchestrator** coordinates **Retrieval**, **Prompt builder**, and **Tool layer**. **Vector store** helps locate indexed content; the application still needs access filtering and evidence about source versions. **Model gateway** selects a provider option. Its branches do not imply that every request calls every model.

Background **AI Workers** also invoke the orchestrator. Enqueuing work establishes acceptance, not completion. The product needs a durable way to distinguish queued, finished, failed, and uncertain work, with duplicate handling appropriate to its queue.

The dashed connections into **Observability** and **Evaluation** join operational evidence to quality review. They cannot make an unsupported summary accurate by themselves. This is a responsibility map, not a requirement to deploy every component for a small feature.
