A proof checker can confirm that a proof follows from a formal statement. People still have to decide whether that statement captures the security property they intended.

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code study used AI agents to prove selected properties of a compiled `libsignal` binary. Researchers reviewed and approved the Lean specification. Proof agents generated proof terms, then Lean's kernel and a comparator checked the proof against that approved target. A wrong or mismatched proof is rejected; a weak specification can still produce a valid proof of a limited claim.

The paper reports about 6.5 million lines of checked proof code, but the proof applies only to the stated model and assumptions. It covers selected properties of a one-to-one `libsignal` command-line build, not every Signal application or device behavior.
