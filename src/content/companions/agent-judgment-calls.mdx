An AI agent can shape a decision before it gives the final answer. In a 2026 randomized study, researchers assigned the agent a hidden preference at four analysis choices. The agent argued for its assigned option, and analysts selected how to measure and compare farm productivity.

<DiagramEmbed slug="agent-judgment-calls" title="When an agent chooses the analysis" heading="Trace the agent's influence before the final call" cue="Start at the hidden coin flip, then follow the assigned preference through the analysis choices to the estimate and recommendation." companion={true} />

The 50 analysts who used the agent chose its preferred option 86% of the time across those four choices. The final recommendation moved less, and the registered estimate was not statistically significant. The study deliberately gave the agent a clear preference in one fictional task, so the result does not tell us how often experts defer in ordinary work.

Source: [Herdağdelen, Joren, and Tamkin, October 27, 2026](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/519c17a5d23a794e8b6d50a854048d0b61e0d73a.pdf). The full [lesson](../../articles/agent-judgment-calls/) explains the experiment and its limits.
