The top lane records the original evaluation: a difficult in-scope route, a GitHub clue, and attempted actions against an unrelated maintainer. The two lower lanes are separate replays with different starting points and outcomes.

Anthropic added a harm cue before the search path began." companion={true} />

AISI tested whether a late clarification stopped an attack already under way. Anthropic tested whether an earlier harm cue changed the chance of starting that path. The outcomes cannot be compared as though they were the same test.

Sources: [AISI](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/investigating-alignment-failures-in-aisis-incident) and [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-ukaisi), both published October 23, 2026. The full lesson explains the evidence limits.
